2. In short

Your game progress stays on your device. Between games, the app shows ads from Google AdMob; personalized ads only if you agree. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not use any analytics or tracking tools. Only if you voluntarily switch on “Play online” does our server store a player name and your online results. You can delete this data yourself at any time.

3. Game progress on your device

Your game progress (e.g. level, Word Trip, achievements, statistics, character, garden and house, settings, purchased items) and an interrupted game are stored only on your device. We have no access to it. You delete this data by clearing the app’s storage in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Afterwards, you can get permanent purchases back via “Restore purchases” in the Store.

4. Android backup

If you have switched on Google backup on your device, Android may store your game progress in your personal backup with Google so that it can be restored on a new device. You control this in your device settings. We do not receive any data in the process.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads only between games, never in the middle of a game: once after the 7th completed level, then only from the 15th completed level onwards, and then at most after every 4th game. In addition, you can voluntarily watch short videos to get a reward (e.g. a “Second Chance”, coins or an Arena ticket). The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). In doing so, Google processes in particular your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, language setting and information about which ads were shown and tapped. Data may also be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Consent: When you first start the app, a Google window (User Messaging Platform) asks you whether ads may be personalized based on your data and whether information may be stored on or read from your device (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) of the German TDDDG). Without consent, the app shows only non-personalized ads where possible. You can change or withdraw your decision at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings for ads. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.

Ad-free: With the “Ad-free” purchase (also included in the Premium Pack and the Starter pack), the app no longer shows any ads; rewards are then given without a video.

More about data processing by Google: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. You enter payment details only with Google; we do not receive any payment details, only the confirmation of which item was purchased. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Playing online (voluntary)

Only if you switch on “Play online” in the app does the app take part in the online features (Weekly league, Arena, daily ranking, online duels, friends, visits to other players’ houses). Our server then stores:

  • your player name, your friend code, the flag you chose and the game language,
  • your profile: your character’s appearance, level, best word, achievements, Showcase, garden and house, whether you have the Gold Path,
  • your results: XP of the week and League placements, Daily Challenge, Arena runs, online duels and Duel rating,
  • your friendships with the record of your duels, visits and “likes” in houses, reports of offensive names,
  • a random device ID with a secret key, so that only your device can change your data.

Other players can see your name, friend code, flag, character, your profile and your points. There is no account with an email address or password, and we do not store IP addresses. To detect cheating, the server checks results and keeps a record of suspicious results.

Storage period: Online duels and Arena runs are deleted after 60 days, suspicious results after 90 days, results of past weeks and days, visits, “likes” and reports after 12 months at the latest. Your player name and your profile remain stored until you delete them.

The server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA; Cloudflare’s privacy policy). Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data when the server is accessed. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time (see section 8).

8. Deleting online data

In the app: Settings → “Stop playing online and delete my data”. This immediately deletes all your online data on the server. Without the app, you can do it via the web page Delete online data.

9. Sharing, invitations and duel links

When you share a result, invite a friend or send a duel, you send the message yourself (e.g. via WhatsApp or SMS). It contains only what you see in it, such as your player name, friend code, points and a link to our website. The duel code is in the link after the # sign and is not transmitted to a server when the link is opened.

10. Reminders

If you allow it, the app schedules reminders on your device (at most one per day), e.g. for the Daily Challenge. They are scheduled only on the device; no data is transmitted to us or to third parties. You switch them off in the app under Settings → Reminders or in the Android settings.

11. Images and website (GitHub Pages)

To keep the app small, it loads large images (e.g. cities and trip stations) from our website nurabos95-sudo.github.io when you view them. The duel links and this policy are hosted there too. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When it is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address (GitHub’s privacy statement). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a small, fast app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). No game data is transmitted, and we do not receive any access data. Without internet, the app shows simpler built-in images.

12. Google Play

When you download the app via Google Play, Google processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We have no influence on this.

13. Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed at children.

14. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent with effect for the future. To exercise these rights, write to us at the email address in section 1; for online data, please include your friend code. For advertising and purchase data, it is best to contact Google directly. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

15. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this policy. The version published here at any given time applies.
