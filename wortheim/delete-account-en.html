Delete online data (“Delete account”) – Wortheim

Last updated: {{DATUM}} · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

1. What is the “account”?

The app has no account with an email address or password. Only players who switch on “Play online” get a player name with a friend code and online results on our server. You can have this online data completely deleted at any time.

2. Deleting in the app (immediately)

  1. Open the app and tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top.
  2. In the “Online rankings” section, tap “Stop playing online and delete my data”.

All your online data is deleted from the server immediately. Your game progress on the device is kept.

3. Deleting without the app (by email)

Don’t have the app anymore? Send an email to the address below with the subject “Delete online data” and include your player name with friend code (e.g. Anna#1234). We will delete your online data within 30 days and confirm this to you by email. If we cannot clearly identify you, we will ask you once for more details.

4. What is deleted

Player name, friend code, flag, profile (character, level, best word, achievements, Showcase, garden and house), results in League, Arena, Daily Challenge and duels, Duel rating, friendships, visits, “likes”, your reports and the device ID with key.

5. What is not deleted

None of this remains on our server. The game progress on your device is not included: you delete it by clearing the app’s storage in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Purchases remain stored with Google Play and can be restored.
